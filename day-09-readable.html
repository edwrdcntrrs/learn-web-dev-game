Field notes from the eastern relay

The relay is drawing power on a channel nobody has used since the Collapse, and it is drawing it steadily, which rules out a fault. Someone replaced the regulator. The solder is fresh.

I have left a marker and a frequency. If they come back to it, they will know where to find us, and they will know that whoever left it could read a schematic.
